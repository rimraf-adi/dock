\section{Introduction}
Developing new cancer drugs takes years and costs billions. Most new drugs fail in clinical trials due to toxicity or resistance. Drug repurposing tests existing drugs with known safety profiles as cancer treatments. Some antiparasitic and anthelmintic drugs show anticancer effects in early studies. For example, benzimidazoles affect microtubules, and ivermectin blocks nuclear import.

Previous computational studies often tested single drugs against single targets. Here, we screened 13 repurposed drugs against 8 cancer targets under uniform conditions. We compared their calculated binding affinities, ligand efficiencies, and contact networks against standard clinical chemotherapies.

\section{Computational Methods}

\subsection{Target Selection and Structural Curation}
Eight high-resolution crystallographic structures were retrieved from the RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{$\beta$-Tubulin (TUBB):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4O2B}{4O2B} (2.30\,\AA) \cite{prota2014}.
    \item \textbf{Importin-$\alpha$ (KPNA2):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4WV6}{4WV6} (1.75\,\AA) \cite{pumroy2015}.
    \item \textbf{p21-Activated Kinase 1 (PAK1):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2HY8}{2HY8} (2.00\,\AA).
    \item \textbf{Glucose Transporter 1 (GLUT1 / SLC2A1):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/5EQI}{5EQI} (3.00\,\AA) \cite{deng2014}.
    \item \textbf{STAT3 SH2 Domain:} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6NJS}{6NJS} (2.70\,\AA) \cite{bai2019}.
    \item \textbf{MDM2 Ubiquitin Ligase:} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4HG7}{4HG7} (1.60\,\AA) \cite{sun2014}.
    \item \textbf{Frizzled-4 CRD (FZD4):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6TFB}{6TFB} (1.68\,\AA).
    \item \textbf{Thioredoxin Reductase 1 (TXNRD1):} PDB \href{https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2ZZB}{2ZZB} (3.20\,\AA).
\end{enumerate}

Water and non-protein atoms were removed. We added polar hydrogens, merged non-polar hydrogens, and assigned Gasteiger charges using OpenBabel. Co-crystallized inhibitors defined the binding pockets.

\subsection{Ligand Preparation and Parameterization}
We downloaded 3D SDF structures for 13 drugs from PubChem. Structures were minimized with the MMFF94 force field. We assigned flexible torsions and charges using Meeko. Ivermectin (MW 875.1\,Da) was constrained to maintain its macrocyclic shape.

\subsection{Docking Protocol}
Docking used AutoDock Vina v1.2.7 \cite{trott2010}. Grid boxes were centered on reference ligands with a 10\,\AA{} buffer. We used an exhaustiveness of 16 for small molecules and 8 for macrocycles.

\subsection{Efficiency Metrics and Contact Mapping}
Physicochemical descriptors were calculated with RDKit:
\begin{align}
    \text{Ligand Efficiency (LE)} &= \frac{-\Delta G}{N_{\text{heavy}}}\quad (\text{kcal}\cdot\text{mol}^{-1}\cdot\text{HA}^{-1}) \\
    \text{Lipophilic Efficiency (LipE)} &= pK_d - \text{cLogP} \approx \frac{-\Delta G}{1.363} - \text{cLogP}
\end{align}
Residue interactions were extracted from the top-scoring pose. We mapped hydrogen bonds ($D \le 3.5$\,\AA) and hydrophobic contacts ($D \le 4.0$\,\AA).

\section{Results}

\subsection{Global Screen Landscape}
We evaluated 96 compound-target complexes. Calculated binding free energies ranged from $-12.17$\,kcal/mol to $+1.22$\,kcal/mol. In total, 42 pairs (43.8\%) scored $\le -7.0$\,kcal/mol, and 17 pairs (17.7\%) scored $\le -8.5$\,kcal/mol (Figure~\ref{fig:heatmap}).

Anthelmintics had the highest median affinity ($-7.85$\,kcal/mol), followed by antifungals ($-7.42$\,kcal/mol), antiparasitics ($-7.05$\,kcal/mol), and antimalarials ($-6.68$\,kcal/mol).

\subsection{Praziquantel: Tubulin and GLUT1 Binding}
Praziquantel (PRA) produced strong docking scores:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Microtubule Binding:} PRA scored $-10.08$\,kcal/mol on $\beta$-tubulin. Mebendazole scored $-9.56$\,kcal/mol, Fenbendazole $-9.10$\,kcal/mol, and Albendazole $-7.22$\,kcal/mol.
    \item \textbf{Transporter Blockade:} PRA docked to GLUT1 with $-9.63$\,kcal/mol.
    \item \textbf{MDM2 Engagement:} PRA bound MDM2 with $-8.82$\,kcal/mol.
    \item \textbf{Ligand Efficiency:} PRA (312.4\,Da, 23 heavy atoms, $\text{cLogP} = 2.53$) yielded $LE = 0.438$\,kcal/mol/HA and $\text{LipE} = 4.86$ on $\beta$-tubulin (Figure~\ref{fig:le}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Broad Polypharmacological Profile of Mebendazole}
Mebendazole (MEB) scored $\le -7.0$\,kcal/mol across all 8 targets (mean $-8.09$\,kcal/mol) (Figure~\ref{fig:radar}). Scores included $\beta$-tubulin ($-9.56$), GLUT1 ($-8.60$), TXNRD1 ($-8.47$), MDM2 ($-8.24$), Frizzled-4 ($-8.03$), Importin-$\alpha$ ($-7.81$), PAK1 ($-7.01$), and STAT3 ($-7.01$).

\subsection{Recapitulation of Known Mechanisms}
The virtual screen matched established binding modes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Niclosamide on STAT3:} Niclosamide bound the STAT3 SH2 domain ($-8.44$\,kcal/mol) with hydrogen bonds to Arg609 (2.68\,\AA), Ser611 (2.70\,\AA), Ser613 (2.80\,\AA), and Ser636 (2.99\,\AA) \cite{bai2019}.
    \item \textbf{Ivermectin on Importin-$\alpha$:} Ivermectin docked to the NLS groove with $-9.47$\,kcal/mol \cite{wagstaff2012}.
    \item \textbf{Physical Discrimination:} Ivermectin scored $+1.22$\,kcal/mol in the tubulin pocket, reflecting expected steric exclusion.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Chemotherapy Benchmark Validation}
We compared calculated scores against standard chemotherapies (Figure~\ref{fig:chemo}, Table~\ref{tab:chemo}). Praziquantel scored $-10.08$\,kcal/mol on $\beta$-tubulin, compared to colchicine at $-7.06$\,kcal/mol. Mebendazole scored $-9.56$\,kcal/mol, compared to nocodazole at $-8.77$\,kcal/mol. On MDM2, praziquantel and mebendazole scored $-8.82$ and $-8.24$\,kcal/mol, while Nutlin-3a scored $-8.39$\,kcal/mol. On STAT3, niclosamide scored $-8.44$\,kcal/mol compared to Stattic at $-5.62$\,kcal/mol.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Therapeutic Rationale for Polypharmacology}
Targeting multiple pathways may limit drug resistance. Dual inhibition of tubulin ($\le -9.5$\,kcal/mol) and GLUT1 ($\le -8.6$\,kcal/mol) could affect cell division and metabolism. Both mebendazole and praziquantel displayed this dual-target binding profile. Additionally, mebendazole's score on MDM2 ($-8.24$\,kcal/mol) offers a potential structural explanation for the p53 accumulation reported in previous studies \cite{guerini2019}.

\subsection{Praziquantel Assessment}
Praziquantel produced the highest calculated tubulin affinity ($-10.08$\,kcal/mol) and ligand efficiency ($0.438$\,kcal/mol/HA) in our screen. Since these are in silico docking scores, physical binding must be confirmed experimentally. However, praziquantel's known safety profile and CNS penetration make it a candidate for further testing in models of glioblastoma.

\subsection{Methodological Considerations}
Limitations of this study include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Rigid Receptor Approximation:} Protein flexibility is not fully captured, which can affect docking scores.
    \item \textbf{Metallodrug Parameterization:} Auranofin contains gold(I). Standard force fields do not accurately model metal coordination, limiting the reliability of these specific scores.
\end{itemize}

\section{Experimental Roadmap}
We recommend the following experimental validations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Molecular Dynamics:} Run explicit-solvent MD on PRA–BTUB, MEB–BTUB, and NIC–STA3 to calculate binding free energies.
    \item \textbf{Microtubule Assembly Turbidimetry:} Test inhibition of tubulin polymerization by praziquantel, mebendazole, and colchicine at 340\,nm.
    \item \textbf{Viability Assays:} Measure $IC_{50}$ of praziquantel in U87MG and U251 cell lines.
    \item \textbf{Surface Plasmon Resonance (SPR):} Measure binding kinetics ($K_D$, $k_{\text{on}}$, $k_{\text{off}}$) for praziquantel against recombinant $\beta$-tubulin and GLUT1.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
This in silico study evaluates repurposed drugs across eight cancer targets. Praziquantel and mebendazole produced strong calculated binding affinities for $\beta$-tubulin and GLUT1. Niclosamide scored highly against STAT3. These computational findings require experimental validation but provide a basis for future laboratory testing.
